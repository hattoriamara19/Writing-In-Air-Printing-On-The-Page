How to Use

  1. Put a small bright green/yellow marker or tape on the pen tip.
  2. Allow camera permission.
  3. Press Start Camera.
  4. Keep the colored pen tip visible to the camera.
  5. Move the pen in the air like you are writing.
  6. The red dot shows the detected pen tip.
  7. The detected movement is converted into writing.
  8. Use Switch Camera to change between front and back cameras.
  9. Press Clear to erase the writing.
  10. Press Save to save your writing.
💡 For best results, use a small bright green or yellow sticker at the pen tip and keep the background reasonably plain.
