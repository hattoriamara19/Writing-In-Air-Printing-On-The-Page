How to Use

  1. Put a small bright green or yellow sticker on the pen tip.
  2. Allow camera permission.
  3. Press Start Camera.
  4. Keep the colored tip visible.
  5. Move the pen slowly in the air.
  6. The red dot shows the detected tip.
  7. The movement is drawn on the writing canvas.
  8. Use Switch Camera for front/back camera.
  9. Use Clear to erase the drawing.
  10. Use Save to save the drawing.
💡 Best results: use a bright green/yellow marker at the pen tip and avoid a background containing similar colors.
