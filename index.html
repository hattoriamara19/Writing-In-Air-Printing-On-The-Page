How to use

1. Use a normal ball pen.

2. Start the front camera.

3. Keep a plain light background.

4. Keep the pen clearly visible.

5. Move the pen tip in the air.

6. The red point shows the estimated pen tip.

7. The movement is transferred to the white writing area.
